## Idea
Child Safe O2 is a vehicle safety system designed to prevent accidents involving children who are accidentally left inside parked vehicles. The system detects the presence of a child after the driver leaves the vehicle and immediately sends an alert to the parent's mobile phone. It also activates a buzzer and starts a compact oxygen and humidification unit to provide temporary support until the child is rescued. The system works even when the vehicle is switched off using a separate power source. It is affordable easy to install and suitable for personal cars school buses and other passenger vehicles. The project aims to improve child safety reduce heatstroke related incidents and provide a practical solution that can help save lives.
